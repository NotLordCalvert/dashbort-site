# Dashbort privacy policy

Dashbort is a personal dashboard that runs on its owner's own device.

- It requests read-only access to the owner's Google Calendar, Google Tasks, Google Drive and Google Analytics, only to display that information on the owner's screen.
- It does not collect, store, sell or share Google user data with anyone. Data is held only on the owner's device.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
